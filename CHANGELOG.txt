HCT 1.6 - changelog
===================

Unreleased
  - The Wikimedia Commons photos of the city tracks (402 pictures and their credits) are no longer included. Their
    slots stay named in each scenery.json, to be filled with free photos (CREDITS.txt).

2.2.3
  - Car selection: the procession andas' top-speed bar is now filled on a walking scale and reads "50 KM/H"
    (on the game's own scale a walking pace was an empty bar).
  - Plaza de Illapel: the PROCESION ILLAPEL arch's board is wide enough for its words.
  - The mod's campaign menus play the game's own music (no third-party music in the mod).

2.2.2
  - The andas lean up to 25 degrees into the bends and hop a little as they walk, rocking forward and back.

2.2.1
  - The andas lean three times as far into the bends.

2.2.0 - the mod readied for publication
  - Campaigns: Chile (126 races), Rutas de Chile (point-to-point stages on real roads), Aventura Chile,
    Competencia Chile 2026 and Grand Prix 2026 (fictional season, needs the Senna Forever DLC).
  - New: the Extremely Large Telescope track on Cerro Armazones - the climb, a lap round the dome and one and a half
    laps inside it, from afternoon into the night.
  - Plaza de Illapel: the town round the plaza from OpenStreetMap's building footprints.
  - The mod's own vehicles (taxi, micro, horse carts, jet skis, procession andas), voices, horns and models.
  - Everything built from open data (OpenStreetMap, SRTM, Sentinel-2) and the mod's own tools; no file of the game.
