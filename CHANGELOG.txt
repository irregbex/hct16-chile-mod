HCT 1.6 - changelog
===================

Fully vibecoded: every version below was made by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic) from one player's directions.
We need your help in refining it - report bugs or send fixes on GitHub (Issues / pull requests).

Track editor (2026-10-25, alongside 2.2.4 - the game plugin is unchanged)
  - The browser track editor is now in the repository (TrackEditor\) and in the release as HCT16-Track-Editor.zip:
    tracks, scenery, 3D assets (.obj import), time of day, weather and campaigns. It finds the installed game in Steam.
  - The ground beside the road takes the colours of each track's own strip textures (sea and sand on the beaches,
    a coast's sections, snow...) instead of plain grass; the right-hand ground of Drive mode is no longer dark.
  - Library thumbnails for the 77 assets that had none.

2.2.4
  - New track "Santiago inundado: La Reina -> La Florida": a jet ski race down the real avenues of Santiago
    (Principe de Gales, Avenida Ossa, Americo Vespucio; OpenStreetMap), the city flooded by a storm - brown, muddy
    water, sunk cars and buses, debris, people waiting on the roofs, rescue boats; the avenues lined three rows deep
    with houses and blocks of flats in each comuna's colours. In the Chile, Rutas de Chile,
    Competencia Chile (bonus race) and Aventura Chile campaigns.
  - Santiago inundado winds far more: S-bends between the drowned blocks, a sway all along, and the water narrows to
    3 lanes and widens to 7 (ground.json TransitionTexture: width changes fade through the track's own strip).
  - Thunderstorms (storm.json): lightning flashes and bolts, synthesised thunder, and muddy spray for the jet skis.
  - Sky recipes: a "Storm" modifier - a dark slate sky, no sun, dim light.
  - The Wikimedia Commons photos of the city tracks (402 pictures and their credits) are no longer included. Their
    slots stay named in each scenery.json, to be filled with free photos (CREDITS.txt).
  - The "Bienvenidos a Illapel" roadside sign (a third-party photo with logos) is no longer included.
  - Every track's road and ground strips redrawn from scratch: only where the road ends and its lanes run, and
    the track's own colours, are kept; asphalt, kerbs, lane lines and the grain of dirt, grass, sand and water are the
    mod's own drawing.

2.2.3
  - Car selection: the procession andas' top-speed bar is now filled on a walking scale and reads "50 KM/H"
    (on the game's own scale a walking pace was an empty bar).
  - Plaza de Illapel: the PROCESION ILLAPEL arch's board is wide enough for its words.
  - The mod's campaign menus play the game's own music (no third-party music in the mod).

2.2.2
  - The andas lean up to 25 degrees into the bends and hop a little as they walk, rocking forward and back.

2.2.1
  - The andas lean three times as far into the bends.

2.2.0 - the mod readied for publication
  - Campaigns: Chile (126 races), Rutas de Chile (point-to-point stages on real roads), Aventura Chile,
    Competencia Chile 2026 and Grand Prix 2026 (fictional season, needs the Senna Forever DLC).
  - New: the Extremely Large Telescope track on Cerro Armazones - the climb, a lap round the dome and one and a half
    laps inside it, from afternoon into the night.
  - Plaza de Illapel: the town round the plaza from OpenStreetMap's building footprints.
  - The mod's own vehicles (taxi, micro, horse carts, jet skis, procession andas), voices, horns and models.
  - Everything built from open data (OpenStreetMap, SRTM, Sentinel-2) and the mod's own tools; no file of the game.
