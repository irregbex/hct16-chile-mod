HCT 1.6 - changelog
===================

Fully vibecoded: every version below was made by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic) from one player's directions.
We need your help in refining it - report bugs or send fixes on GitHub (Issues / pull requests).

2.4.4 (2026-11-08)
  - Retro campaigns: the minimap shows a chequered flag across the start/finish line and a small red circle where the
    pits are (race.json PitSign; tracks without a pit show none).

2.4.3 (2026-11-08)
  - The retro-campaign engine is fully data-driven and campaign-neutral: no campaign, car or file name is built into
    the code any more. New campaign.json keys "RetroCarLooks" (each car's HUD colour and qualification pictures),
    "RetroSharpTextures", "QualifyJingle" and "QualifyBackground"; new campaigns\<folder>\texts.json for a campaign's
    own texts (car names, ...); environment presets retro_day / retro_night / retro_day_to_night / retro_night_to_day.
    The keys are listed in Content\HCT15Content\campaigns\README.txt.
  - The retro sprite cars are no longer built in (define sprite cars in cars\streetcars.json).

2.4.2 (2026-11-08) - the jet ski expansion, part 1
  - Three beaches become harbour laps (same rounds and map places, 3 laps each): Port of Valparaiso (round 124),
    Port of San Antonio (121) and Quintero Bay (118). Container ships, a bulk carrier and an LNG tanker moored along
    the straights, quays with gantry cranes and container stacks, tugs, a breakwater with its lighthouse, sea lions and
    gulls (14 new assets: buque_*, granelero_*, metanero_*, remolcador, contenedores_pila, muelle_puerto).
  - "Cuadrado de Playa Amarilla" (round 123): 3 laps of a rounded square round four giant buoys, at night, lit only by
    some 200 blinding LED boards and gantries of invented betting brands, LED planes and yachts with invented ads
    (race.json NightVeil darkens everything but the screens).
  - "Rescate en Las Docas" (round 116): humans only, a 2-minute clock, out to sea to rescue the princess from an
    inflatable castle past sharks, waterspouts and sea lions (crossers.json "Hazard"); miss the clock and the castle
    deflates (race.json HumansOnly / TimeLimit).
  - New round 165, "Laguna de San Alfonso del Mar": 3 laps round the giant seawater pool of Algarrobo.
  - Engine: retro-style campaign options (campaign.json "RetroRules": solid roadside objects, fixed-speed CPU, simple
    pits, a split-screen-aware HUD and intro); no campaign in this release uses them yet.
  - The installer retires the old beach-wave files of the five changed beaches.

2.4.0 (2026-11-05)
  - Plaza de Illapel: 10 racers on the grid (players included), set by the track's race.json "Racers". Four new andas -
    Our Lady of Candlemas, Our Lady of La Tirana, St Rose of Lima and St Peter - make ten, each with its own top speed,
    start, grip and tank (47-56 km/h); every CPU anda is a different one (they are dealt from a shuffled deck, the
    players' own left out), and every screen sees the same anda for the same rival.
  - Chile campaign: the coins on the road are Chilean 500-peso coins (asset moneda_500, both faces drawn for the mod,
    saturated gold) that spin and shine; campaign.json "CoinModel" picks the coin of a campaign, a car's own CoinModel
    (the carretas' carrots) still wins.
  - Chile map: the region tabs and the stage list say "Region IV - Coquimbo (10)" (worldmap.json "RegionNumbers"), and
    a region's stages are listed in round order.
  - Playa Las Docas and Playa Amarilla: the beach widens into bays and narrows to rocky points, with rock walls, rocks
    in the shallows and reefs just past the buoys (scenery.json; the dressed beach kept as scenery_playas_base.json).

2.3.9 (2026-11-05)
  - Fixed: on Plaza de Illapel with the andas nobody could win - every rival walked at the player's own pace, so after a
    few laps everybody arrived together. Each anda now has its own top speed (48-55 km/h, the lighter ones quicker,
    the heavier ones steadier with a bigger tank), and each rival walks at its own anda's speed.

2.3.8 (2026-11-05)
  - Rutas de Chile > Los Vilos - Illapel: its three toll plazas are taken out (kept aside in scenery_peajes_quitados.json
    and tolls_quitados.json, to put back later).
  - Win stickers back to their original size (1.5x was still too big).
  - F-150: the chase camera further back again.

2.3.7 (2026-11-05)
  - Jet ski stunts: a clean landing gives half a nitro (the game's own boost, half the car's nitro time, no charge spent).
  - Podium: every car arrives with its own engine sound (the game's engine of that car), no longer one shared loop.
  - Podium: the Chilean flags wave and have their white star (the crowd's flags and La Moneda's).
  - Podium: a parked jet ski no longer trails its white wake streaks.
  - Win stickers at 1.5x their old size (2x was too much), still growing towards their own side.
  - F-150: the chase camera a little further back.

2.3.6 (2026-11-05)
  - Fixed: Rutas de Chile > Plaza de Illapel opened a car selection with no anda and the game hung; a round that names
    its cars can always race them.
  - Podium: someone else hands the cup over - a presenter picks it up from its stand, walks it to the winner and holds it
    out; the winner takes it and lifts it.
  - Podium: a jet ski's winner is its own rider, in his wetsuit, life vest and helmet colours.
  - Playa Luna's podium: the whole crowd as the beach's bathers (black bars).
  - The end-of-race stickers are twice as big, each growing towards its own side.
  - The F-150's chase camera sits a little further back (a fifth of the micro's pull-back).

2.3.5 (2026-11-03)
  - Podium: a vehicle's own visible driver gets off and takes the prize - the carreta's huaso leaves his seat in his
    poncho and hat (the model marks him as OBJ group "driver"; any model can). The game's own cars have their drivers
    painted into the body: for François (both motorbikes) and Breeze (the co-driver stays) the podium reads the car from
    your installed game, takes the driver's pieces out and he or she gets off in the colours of that variant; The Fuzz
    sends a police officer in uniform and cap. Which cars and where their driver sits:
    Content\HCT15Content\podium\drivers.json (text).
  - Podium: the micro's driver is a stout bald man, the taxi driver a stout man with short black hair; one of an anda's bearers
    leaves it and takes the cup (the image stays on the anda); the andas park straight and level, out of their race lean.
  - Plaza de Illapel: 3 laps (was 5); the andas pick up big glasses of water instead of fuel cans.
  - Podium music no longer cuts out: the race behind the ceremony (hooves, horns, taunts) stays quiet while it shows.
  - The carreta's right speech bubble no longer jumps up and down.

2.3.4 (2026-11-03)
  - Podium: a carreta gallops in on hooves (an anda is marched in by its bearers) instead of an engine's sound.
  - Jet skis: the chunks thrown up behind them are white sea foam (they were the cars' dirt particles, made brown by
    the beach's sunset light).
  - Speech bubbles on the screen's edge hold still (a dead band on the on-screen correction).
  - "Más bencina" (Race\Agregados): a new mix of the song.

2.3.3 (2026-11-03)
  - Beach podium: the crowd in swimsuits and bikinis, the promo models in bikinis of their platform colours, the staff in
    shorts, tees and sunglasses; the riders in a wetsuit and a life vest of their colour (their jet ski's own rider
    gets off with them). Umbrellas, towels and surfboards on the sand, palms, a lifeguard tower, beach balls bouncing over

  - Toll plazas are switched off for now (the booths stay as scenery); their option in Options > General is greyed out.
  - A long speech bubble no longer flickers between two places.

2.3.2 (2026-11-03)
  - Beach podium: when jet skis finish in the top three, they arrive over the sea at sunset and the stage stands on the
    sand (waves, the low sun's road of light on the water, spray); on the flooded Santiago, muddy water.
  - The podium's drivers get out in racing helmets, take them off by the car (set on its roof) - long, full 80s hair -
    and walk over with knees that bend; a close camera shot shows it.
  - Playa Luna's intro is a sunny summer afternoon (blue sky, turquoise sea with waves breaking on the shore, golden
    sand); its sea no longer turns dark blue for a few frames while the race loads.
  - The pink marker over fuel cans, nitros and coins is gone (a material-less part of the game's pickups).
  - Car selection: no more floating light glints in front of / behind the jet skis, carts and andas; the andas are full
    size with one player and smaller only in split screen (streetcars.json "SelectScale2P").
  - Road traffic is switched off for now (it could make the stage impossible to win); its option in Options > General is
    shown greyed out.
  - The old flat frames of the Playa Luna intro are no longer shipped (the installer removes them).

2.3.1 (2026-11-03)
  - Options > General has a new row, "Road traffic" (yes / no): no switches off the traffic of the road stages of
    Rutas de Chile, so only the rivals are on the road. It applies from the next race; saved in
    HCT15Content\hct15.settings.json ("RoadTraffic").
  - A track's traffic.json can name the colours its traffic wears ("Colours": colour ids of cars\streetcars.json, each
    traffic car one of them at random) - so traffic of the same model as the rivals never looks like one of them.
  - The mod's own 13 songs now come with it (HCT15Music\Race\Agregados and Race\Illapel, OGG): the Chile campaigns
    play them.
  - New podium music: a 61-second cut of one of those songs, its big hit when the champion arrives.

2.3.0 (2026-11-03)
  - Podium ceremony: after every race of the mod's campaigns a one-minute live 3D show comes before the results. The
    3rd, 2nd and 1st arrive from the road behind the stage in their own cars (copies of the race's models, the mod's
    trucks, buses and horse carts too - the horses trot), with real braking, a turn, the ramp and suspension; promo
    models receive them, presenters announce the real names and times, photographers' flashes light the scene; CO2,
    metallic confetti and fireworks for the champion, who lifts the cup; champagne; six rows of fans on each side jump,
    each at their own rhythm, a third of them waving Chilean flags; kisses with rising hearts, tears, signs; a broadcast
    camera cuts between shots; then the results table. The sky, the horizon and the scenery come from the track and its
    climate. Behind the LED wall on the Santiago tracks: Costanera Center, La Moneda lit up with its flag, Torre Entel and
    rows of towers. Enter / Space / gamepad A skip it. Not after a race lost on fuel.
  - Everything of it is editable with free tools: HCT15Content\podium\podio.json (captions, times, what stands behind
    the screen per track, any OBJ model of the library - edit them in Blender) and the sounds one file each in
    HCT15Content\podium\sonidos (music, crowd, engine, cheer, camera shutter, fireworks, CO2, cork; see its README.txt).
  - Playa Luna's intro is now a live 3D scene instead of the flat animation: a jet ski along the shore, another jumping a
    wave over the bathers in slow motion, an umbrella flying off, the pack roaring past and the title.
  - Santiago - Estación Central no longer has fireworks during the race (they belong to the podium); the installer
    removes the old file.

2.2.5 (2026-10-28)
  - Santiago inundado now ends at the Estadio Nacional: from La Reina by Principe de Gales, Ossa and Vespucio to the
    Grecia roundabout, west on Avenida Grecia and south on Pedro de Valdivia (9.8 km, was 12.7 km to La Florida).
    Renamed "Santiago inundado: La Reina -> Estadio Nacional" in every campaign and on the maps.
  - The finish is inside the flooded covered courts of the Estadio Nacional's polideportivo: an arched roof with
    pennants, basketball hoops, bleachers on both sides packed with children jumping and cheering under falling
    confetti, and more children in rubber rings by the doors.
  - Rounder and less abrupt: the street corners are wide arcs, five long S-bends replace the nine tight zigzags, and
    the water changes width by at most two lanes at a time.
  - Street signs for Avenida Grecia and Avenida Pedro de Valdivia.
  - The track's real buildings and trees along Grecia and Pedro de Valdivia are still to come (OpenStreetMap was
    unreachable); the street walls there are generated houses for now.

Track editor (2026-10-25, alongside 2.2.4 - the game plugin is unchanged)
  - The browser track editor is now in the repository (TrackEditor\) and in the release as HCT16-Track-Editor.zip:
    tracks, scenery, 3D assets (.obj import), time of day, weather and campaigns. It finds the installed game in Steam.
  - The ground beside the road takes the colours of each track's own strip textures (sea and sand on the beaches,
    a coast's sections, snow...) instead of plain grass; the right-hand ground of Drive mode is no longer dark.
  - Library thumbnails for the 77 assets that had none.

2.2.4
  - New track "Santiago inundado: La Reina -> La Florida": a jet ski race down the real avenues of Santiago
    (Principe de Gales, Avenida Ossa, Americo Vespucio; OpenStreetMap), the city flooded by a storm - brown, muddy
    water, sunk cars and buses, debris, people waiting on the roofs, rescue boats; the avenues lined three rows deep
    with houses and blocks of flats in each comuna's colours. In the Chile, Rutas de Chile,
    Competencia Chile (bonus race) and Aventura Chile campaigns.
  - Santiago inundado winds far more: S-bends between the drowned blocks, a sway all along, and the water narrows to
    3 lanes and widens to 7 (ground.json TransitionTexture: width changes fade through the track's own strip).
  - Thunderstorms (storm.json): lightning flashes and bolts, synthesised thunder, and muddy spray for the jet skis.
  - Sky recipes: a "Storm" modifier - a dark slate sky, no sun, dim light.
  - The Wikimedia Commons photos of the city tracks (402 pictures and their credits) are no longer included. Their
    slots stay named in each scenery.json, to be filled with free photos (CREDITS.txt).
  - The "Bienvenidos a Illapel" roadside sign (a third-party photo with logos) is no longer included.
  - Every track's road and ground strips redrawn from scratch: only where the road ends and its lanes run, and
    the track's own colours, are kept; asphalt, kerbs, lane lines and the grain of dirt, grass, sand and water are the
    mod's own drawing.

2.2.0 - the mod readied for publication
  - Campaigns: Chile (126 races), Rutas de Chile (point-to-point stages on real roads), Aventura Chile,
    Competencia Chile 2026 and Grand Prix 2026 (fictional season, needs the Senna Forever DLC).
  - New: the Extremely Large Telescope track on Cerro Armazones - the climb, a lap round the dome and one and a half
    laps inside it, from afternoon into the night.
  - Plaza de Illapel: the town round the plaza from OpenStreetMap's building footprints.
  - The mod's own vehicles (taxi, micro, horse carts, jet skis, procession andas), voices, horns and models.
  - Everything built from open data (OpenStreetMap, SRTM, Sentinel-2) and the mod's own tools; no file of the game.
