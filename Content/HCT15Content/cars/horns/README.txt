HCT 1.6 - car horns
===================
<car id>\*.wav or *.ogg    the horn sounds of that car (cl_micro, cl_taxi, ...): one is picked at random,
                           never the same twice in a row, each time the horn button goes down (Right Shift,
                           or the pad's touch pad / Select / View button). A car without a folder honks like
                           the car it speaks with (streetcars.json Taunts: the micro's colours -> cl_micro),
                           else like its donor. Read at start-up. Keep them short (a blast, 1-3 s).
