Santiago inundado: La Reina -> Estadio Nacional - Chile campaign (and Rutas de Chile, Competencia Chile, Aventura Chile),
point to point, jet skis only. The streets of a Santiago flooded by the rain, full of mud, thunder and lightning.
race.json      the real avenues from OpenStreetMap (Príncipe de Gales, Ossa, Américo Vespucio, Grecia, 8.4 km), then
               2 laps round the flooded pitch of the Estadio Nacional (a concert on it) and 2 round the courts of
               its polideportivo; raced to 0.676 of the loop (the rest is a return leg never raced); 5 ramps
road_strip.png brown water from edge to edge, the drowned lane lines and curbs (drawn by the tool, nothing of the game)
storm.json     lightning, thunder and mud spray (HCT15.Storm, plugin 2.2.4); weather.json the downpour;
environment    a dark storm day (overcast)
scenery.json   the OSM buildings and trees + the flood: sunk cars and a bus, debris, people on the roofs, rescue
               boats, street lamps, traffic lights, the avenues' and comunas' signs, Line 4's metro entrances;
               the stadium's stands packed with fans, the concert on the pitch; the polideportivo's hall and
               courts full of basketball pupils in reflective orange and children celebrating
Regenerate:    see the header of Tools\stgo_inundado.py
