PODIUM SOUNDS (HCT 1.6, 2.3.0)
==============================
Every sound of the podium ceremony is one file in this folder. To change one, replace the file with another of THE
SAME NAME (WAV: PCM 16 or 24-bit, or 32-bit float; mono or stereo; any sample rate). No code to touch. A missing file
is simply silent. All of them are original, synthesised for the mod: replace or publish them freely.

FILE                    WHEN IT PLAYS                                                         CURRENT LENGTH
musica.wav              the music of the whole ceremony, from second 0. It is timed: intro    61 s
                        (0-7 s), build (7-29.5), drum roll (29.5-33.5), the "drop" when the
                        champion arrives (33.5), ending (57.5-61). A replacement should last
                        ~61 s with its big hit at second 33.5.
publico_loop.wav        the crowd's murmur and cheering, LOOPED all the time; louder at each  4 s (loop)
                        arrival and with the champion.
motor_loop.wav          each arriving car's engine, LOOPED; the game changes its pitch with   2 s (loop)
                        the speed (Doppler) and its volume with the distance to the camera.
                        Record it at an even, medium pitch (~116 Hz).
ovacion.wav             a cheer when the 3rd, the 2nd and the champion park.                  1.6 s
obturador.wav           every photographer's flash (many times).                              0.3 s
fuego_artificial.wav    every firework burst (from the champion's arrival on).                2 s
co2.wav                 the CO2 jets (the champion's arrival and the cup lift).               2 s
corcho.wav              the champagne cork (second 42.6).                                     0.6 s

The texts, times and buildings of the podium are in ..\podio.json (any text editor too).
