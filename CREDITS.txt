HCT 1.6 - credits and licences
==============================

Who made it - FULLY VIBECODED
  HCT 1.6 is FULLY VIBECODED: all of its code, and its tracks, models, textures and sounds, were made by an AI
  (Claude, by Anthropic) from one player's directions in plain language, using the open data credited below.
  Nobody wrote or reviewed the code by hand.
  WE NEED YOUR HELP IN REFINING IT: bug reports, fixes, better tracks and free photos are all welcome - open an Issue
  or a pull request on the GitHub repository.

Photographs
  No photographs are included. The roadside photo boards of the city tracks are left as open slots: each track's
  scenery.json still names them (Kind "photo", the place's name, "Photo": "photos/NN.jpg" in tracks\<track>\photos\)
  for anyone who wants to fill them. Contributions are welcome: please send only photos you took yourself or that
  carry a free licence, with their author and licence. No logos or coats of arms are used.
  The San Rafael Cathedral of Illapel (model catedral_illapel, and parroquia_san_rafael in the Illapel city-sights track)
  was modelled after the photo "20210103-001 Catedral Illapel frontis detalle.jpg", Ariel Mauricio Malla Gallardo,
  CC BY-SA 4.0 (Wikimedia Commons); the models are published under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the photo itself is not included).
  The Extremely Large Telescope track (cl_obs_armazones) and its elt_* models were drawn from the dimensions published
  by ESO and the colours of its construction photos (Credit: ESO, CC BY 4.0 - potw2430a/b/c, potw2506a, potw2520a at
  https://www.eso.org/public/images/); the photos are not included. The track is not associated with ESO.
  The buildings round the Plaza de Illapel come from OpenStreetMap's building footprints (ODbL, above).

Images, skies and sounds of our own
  The road and ground textures (road_strip.png, ground_*.png), the skies and weather (assets\env\*.png), the maps,
  cards and campaign logos were drawn with the mod's own tools. The skies are put together in the game: the mod takes
  the daytime sky of the player's own copy of the game and applies its own palette to it (sunset, night, dawn).
  The sound effects (neighs, the micro's horn, gallop, the bearers' steps, the bell, the whistle, fireworks) are
  synthesised by the mod or its tools.

Voices
  The voices of the speech bubbles (taunts_sfx\) were generated for this mod with speech synthesis: ElevenLabs and, for
  the anda, XTTS-v2 (Coqui, non-commercial licence) from the voice of one of the mod's contributors, with consent.
  No voice imitates a famous person.

Grand Prix 2026
  The campaign uses the cars of the game's own Senna Forever DLC (you must own it). Teams, drivers, chassis and
  engines are fictional; it is not associated with Formula 1 or any real team or driver. The circuits are named by
  their city only.
